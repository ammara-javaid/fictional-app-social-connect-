Campaign Goal Setting Task 2 of 3
SMART Goals
Specific
Promote the Social Connect app to urban 18–35 year olds via Instagram, TikTok & LinkedIn with targeted ad creatives and organic content.
Measurable
Achieve 500 app downloads, 20% increase in brand awareness (tracked via reach & impressions), 5% engagement rate on posts.
Achievable
Realistic for a 3-week campaign using a mix of organic content (5+ posts) and a mock/small-budget paid ad campaign on 2 platforms.
Relevant
Directly supports the app's growth goal of expanding its user base and increasing visibility among the target demographic.
Time-bound
All goals to be achieved within the 3-week campaign window (Week 1 planning, Week 2 execution, Week 3 analysis).
KPIs & Targets
Reach / Impressions Target: 50,000 impressions across all channels in 3 weeks
Engagement Rate Target: ≥5% (likes, comments, shares, saves)
Click-through Rate (CTR) Target: ≥2% on paid ads
App Downloads / Conversions Target: 500 downloads by end of Week 3
Follower Growth Target: +300 followers on primary channels
Cost Per Install (CPI) Target: ≤$1.50 per install (mock budget benchmark)
Content Strategy Task 3 of 3
Social media posts (5+)
Post 1 — Launch announcement graphic: Bold visual introducing Social Connect. Caption: "Connection reimagined. Social Connect is here. 🚀 Download link in bio."
Post 2 — Feature carousel: 5-slide carousel showcasing key app features (groups, messaging, events, discovery, privacy).
Post 3 — User testimonial quote: Fictional quote-style post — "I found my whole creative community on Social Connect." — @userhandle
Post 4 — Engagement poll / question: "What's missing from your current social apps?" — interactive story poll.
Post 5 — Short Reel/TikTok: 15–30 sec video: "POV: You finally found a social app that actually connects you." trending audio + on-screen text.
Ad creatives (2+) & long-form
Ad 1 — Instagram/Facebook feed ad: Clean 1080×1080 graphic. Headline: "Connect Deeper. Download Social Connect." CTA button: "Get the App."
Ad 2 — Stories/TikTok vertical ad: 9:16 format. Animated text over lifestyle footage. Headline: "Your people are waiting." CTA: swipe up to install.
Blog post: "Why Meaningful Connections Matter in the Digital Age" — 500–700 words, SEO-optimised, with CTA to download the app.
Email newsletter: Subject: "Introducing Social Connect — The App That Gets You." Covers app benefits, screenshots, download CTA, and referral offer.
